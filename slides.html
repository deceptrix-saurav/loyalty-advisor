Introduce yourself and the course. One sentence: this app tells a shop owner whether a loyalty program will pay for itself, and designs one if it will.

The problem: everyone says "start a loyalty program"

Who has it: owners of cafes, kirana stores, salons, boutiques, online shops.

How they decide today:

  • copy the shop next door
  • trust an app vendor's calculator
  • ask a chatbot that invents a number

What nobody checks: does the extra profit beat all the rewards given? Most rewards go to people who would have bought anyway.

Example cafe, from the app's own engine:
gains ₹1,846/month if members spend 6% more, but loses ₹1,059/month (₹12,708/year) if they spend only 2% more.

Where the idea came from: my airport loyalty study

Aeroknots at Delhi Airport: about 70 million passengers a year, 400+ shops.

  • Shops already paid 22 to 38% of sales in fees and revenue share
  • The program added 1.1% (earn only), 2.0% (earn + burn) or 10% (burn only) per bill
  • Much buying is impulsive, so the sales lift was small
  • Nobody had settled who pays for the points; customers redeemed at a few "winner" shops
  • The vendor's model lacked the detail to decide, yet commitments were made
Lesson: the same mistakes happen in small shops, where nobody is paid to catch them. This app is that check.

How it works

Code does the maths, the verdict and every check
AI reads messy text, chooses extra checks, explains, personalises
Budget guard caps tokens, calls, time and rate on every AI call

Demo: one real run, step by step

"I run a small cafe. About 100 customers a day, average bill 250 rupees. We save phone numbers on WhatsApp."

  1. Guard: accepted, 0 tokens
  2. Intake (AI): cafe, 3,000 bills a month, ₹250 bill, phone numbers
  3. Engine: 6 of 6 checks pass; best fit Stamp card (100), then Paid membership (93), Points on spend (91)
  4. Analyst (AI): compares top designs, looks up a principle
  5. Strategist (AI): Pilot first: break-even lift 3.95%, expected ₹1,846/month, pessimistic -₹1,059
  6. Designer (AI): 10 stamps, reward worth ₹80, scripts, 12-week plan
  7. Output guard: every figure checked

How cost was kept down

  • Maths is code, so model calls stay short
  • Form-only runs skip the Intake call
  • Same facts reuse a saved answer (0 tokens)
  • Lean mode skips the Analyst's model calls
  • Long identical rules first, so the prompt cache hits
  • Short structured outputs; tools bound to the profile
  • Hard caps: 30,000 tokens per run, 200,000 per client a day
